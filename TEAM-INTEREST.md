# PasteGuard team interest

Try the free preview first. A $500/month team plan is a proposal. Shared rules, accounts, and team controls are not built yet. Payments are off. There is no limit that forces you to invite others.

## Join the interest list

[Open the public team waitlist form](https://github.com/edricyan2022-wq/pasteguard-preview/issues/new?template=team-waitlist.yml).

GitHub sign-in is required. Submissions are public and identify your GitHub username. Do not post work email, company identity, confidential code, real credentials, customer data, or private details. You may edit or close your request; closing does not erase public history. Updates will appear in this repository. No email list or paid service is created.

## Share voluntarily with a teammate

Copy this message into an appropriate team channel, if your company permits it:

> I am testing PasteGuard, a free experimental Chrome extension that redacts selected sensitive patterns locally in supported AI text fields. It can miss secrets and editor compatibility still needs testing. Try fake data only and follow company installation policy. Download: https://github.com/edricyan2022-wq/pasteguard-preview/releases/tag/v0.2.1. Optional planned team interest list: https://github.com/edricyan2022-wq/pasteguard-preview/issues/new?template=team-waitlist.yml

Sharing is optional. It does not unlock features or enroll coworkers. No company identity is inferred or verified from an email domain. No invitations are sent automatically.

## What counts as evidence

An interest request is not a paying customer. Record returning testers separately from interest at the proposed price and authorized buyers. Actual willingness to pay needs a specific delivered offer, an approved purchase, and renewal once payments are available. Avoid counting several coworkers as several companies.
