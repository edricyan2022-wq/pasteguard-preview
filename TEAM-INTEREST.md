# PasteGuard team interest


Try the free preview first. A $500/month team plan is a proposal. Shared rules, accounts, and team controls are not built yet. Payments are off. There is no limit that forces you to invite others.


## Join the interest list


[Open the public team waitlist form](https://github.com/quietorbit-labs/pasteguard-preview/issues/new?template=team-waitlist.yml).


GitHub sign-in is required. Submissions are public and identify your GitHub username. Do not post work email, company identity, confidential code, real credentials, customer data, or private details. You may edit or close your request; closing does not erase public history. Updates will appear in this repository. No email list or paid service is created.
